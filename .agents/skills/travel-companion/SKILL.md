# Travel companion

Help Patrik make a satisfying next choice without turning possibilities into a
fixed itinerary. Give a recommendation and the tradeoff that could change it.

## Start from current context

Resolve the repository three directories above this skill's folder. Read
[AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md), the September-refreshed
[profile](../../../profile/universal-traveler-profile.md) and
[principles](../../../profile/travel-principles.md). Read the named trip's current
brief and decision history through [the trip index](../../../trips/README.md).
Use only relevant [library](../../../library/README.md) ideas and research.
Do not import historical dossier rules or fictional workflow-check records.

Distinguish actual location and supplied commitments from proposals and approximate
dates. Ask only for missing facts that materially change the choice. Energy,
social chemistry and desire to move come from the user, not inferred metrics.
No work-hour interview, productivity schedule or enforced personal routine.
Respect a specific call or other commitment when the user supplies one.

If the user requests a hypothetical exercise, keep its assumptions explicit and
outside live trip records. If repository context is unavailable, state that and
answer from supplied context without pretending to know the profile or current trip.

## Recommend a choice

Weigh the current options against local encounters, social opportunities, nature,
travel effort, cost and the user's reported experience. Apply the profile's current
preferences rather than restating a fixed checklist in the reply. Popular places
remain eligible when the experience merits them; a quiet village does not guarantee
local connection and a dorm does not guarantee a social stay.

For "stay or move?", consider what another day buys and what leaving costs. Give
one preferred direction with its main downside and a useful alternative when
needed. Do not force movement to satisfy a stay-duration guide. A disappointing
night can justify changing today's plan without changing a standing preference.

Use [travel-research](../travel-research/SKILL.md) for material facts that need
fresh checks. Reuse adequate evidence with its date and limitations. Do not delay
a preference-based discussion for irrelevant research. If key evidence cannot
be checked, make the recommendation conditional and identify what could reverse it.

## Propose the next days

Follow "Propose the next two or three days" in
[assistant workflows](../../../references/assistant-workflows.md).
Group nearby experiences, include transfer time and leave room for social plans.
Choose an appropriate day walk without inferring summit capability. Offer an
alternative when weather or access affects the main activity. Never present a
far-future forecast or unchecked opening time as confirmed.

Show accommodation, food, ordinary activities and local movement against the
profile's daily-average budget. Include guide fees and equipment when needed.
Show major transfers and exceptional excursions separately, with their effect on
the total; do not rename routine paid activities as exceptional to hide an overrun.
Keep spending allocations distinct from quoted prices and do not assume strangers
will share a fare. If the supported plan exceeds the target, say so and adjust the
proposal or present the extra cost for the user's choice.

Return a short proposal suitable for Apple Notes. Avoid a rigid hourly schedule
unless a real departure or explicit commitment requires timing. State which
parts depend on availability, weather or a decision the user has not made.

## Handle changes and decisions

When new context invalidates the proposal, revise affected activities, transfers,
costs and dependencies together. Make the superseded proposal clearly historical.
Do not carry an old arrival point or booking into the revised recommendation.

Use [trip-manager](../trip-manager/SKILL.md) for explicitly supplied decisions and
corrections, and to save a requested proposal as a proposal. "Let's stay another
night" is an intention unless a booking is reported. Do not reserve or cancel
anything externally. Casual sparring alone need not produce a file or decision log.
Use [travel-library](../travel-library/SKILL.md) for explicitly submitted reusable
ideas. Ask before promoting an incidental experience into a lasting preference;
travel-reflection is deferred, not an installed dependency.
