## Recommendation

Keep **Taghazout and Imsouane as competing social-base candidates**, then combine
one with Imlil for mountain and village time. Do not commit to both coastal places
just to complete a list. Imsouane now has enough specific social evidence to merit
equal consideration; the earlier “optional surf duplication” framing was too weak.
Tafraoute remains a promising quieter alternative, with more unresolved access and
social-accommodation questions. These are fit judgments, not measured town rankings.

## Budget implications

Roof House advertises a six-bed mixed dorm at **200 MAD per night**. Tagtout Auberge
in Tafraoute advertises rooms at **280 MAD per night**; do not mistake that for a dorm
bed price. Neither is a quote for your dates, and inclusions and taxes need checking.
[Roof House rates](https://roofhousetaghazout.com/rooms/),
[Tagtout rooms](https://tagtout.com/fr/chambres/).

Easy Going's advertised seven-night dorm/shared-double surf-lesson package is
**€392 per person**, including seven breakfasts, six dinners and five lessons.
That is **€56 per night before lunches and transport**, so it exceeds your €40 daily
target. Its seven-night rental package is **€246**, about **€35.14 per night**, with
seven breakfasts, six dinners and board/wetsuit rental, but no listed lessons.
That leaves only about €4.86 per day for everything else and one uncovered dinner.
The three-night rental package is €111, or €37 per night. These are advertised
packages, not availability checks. [Operator's package details](https://easygoingmorocco.com/).

Recommendation: compare ordinary dorm stays and occasional paid activities before
buying a package. Rental-only pricing does not establish that independent surfing
is suitable for you. The evidence does not yet establish a complete €40 daily
budget for any base; food, guides and date-specific bed prices remain gaps. Daily
lessons count within ordinary spending, not as an invisible exceptional excursion.

## Transport evidence and failure points

- **Coastal connection exists as an advertised service.** Golden Bus lists
  Taghazout–Imsouane at €12, and Marrakech–Taghazout at €25. It provides a booking
  request form, not observed seat inventory. These are useful route leads; exact
  pickup, travel-date operation, booking deadline and last departure remain
  unconfirmed. [Operator listing](https://goldenbusmorocco.com/bus-shuttle/).
- **Do not navigate using the Souk to Surf homepage alone.** Its Imsouane–Taghazout
  summary says 1h50 at 09:30/14:40, while its timetable says 4h15 at 11:15/16:15.
  The linked Rezdy booking page could not be retrieved in this pass. Neither set
  of times is accepted as reliable. [Conflicting operator page](https://souk2surf.com/).
- **Tafraoute has a better bus lead, not a verified bus.** marKoub's Tafraoute–Agadir
  page advertises 50 MAD and roughly 5h15–5h30, naming Jana Viajes and Sahara Prestige.
  However, its introductory copy names a different city pair. Treat the prices,
  duration and operators as leads to check against dated results; do not reverse
  the direction or infer a CTM service. [Booking-platform route page](https://markoub.ma/en/journey/tafraoute_agadir/246_2).
- **Imlil needs a local access check.** A local trekking provider describes shared
  taxis via Asni and private transfers; its guide dates to June 2025. This improves
  the route concept but does not establish today's stand, fare or last connection.
  Conflicting secondary guides were not promoted to confirmed logistics.
  [Atlas Peaks transport guide](https://atlaspeaksimlil.com/marrakech-to-imlil-how-to-get-there/).

Practical fallback policy: if a connection cannot be confirmed, keep an overnight
buffer in the departure town or obtain an agreed private-transfer quote. Those are
planning options, not checked availability or prices. No tightly connected same-day
route is recommended from the evidence above.

## What would change this recommendation

The strongest countercase is to choose Easy Going in Imsouane instead of Taghazout:
its guest testimony fits your shared-meal and solo-traveler priorities directly.
Conversely, the rental/lesson package costs could make a flexible Taghazout dorm
stay preferable. Neither inference proves one town is better overall.

Once you select an arrival airport and a first base, check two or three actual
nights, mixed-dorm availability, meal prices and the corresponding journey. Before
an Imlil hike, check the specific route, guide cost, conditions and short-range
forecast. This pass did not validate hiking fitness, weather, holiday effects or
a two-to-three-day itinerary. Stop broad comparison here: date-specific checks and
local confirmations will add more value than another general destination guide.
