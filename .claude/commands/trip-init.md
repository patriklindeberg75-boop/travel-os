# Start or update a trip

Read [the shared instructions](../../AGENTS.md) and follow
[Start or update a trip](../../references/assistant-workflows.md#start-or-update-a-trip).
Treat `$ARGUMENTS` as the supplied trip context, not a required positional format.

Find an existing trip in `trips/README.md` before creating a folder. Preserve
approximate dates, tentative plans, and unknown facts. Missing dates, a theme,
a viability assessment, or profile files do not block capture. Do not supply
legacy work-hour, budget, or weather defaults.

Read back the changed records and report their paths. Do not start a destination
check, timing handoff, or dossier unless Patrik requests that work.
