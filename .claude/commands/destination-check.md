# Check a destination

Read [the shared instructions](../../AGENTS.md) and follow
[Research a practical question](../../references/assistant-workflows.md#research-a-practical-question).
Treat `$ARGUMENTS` as the destination question and any supplied dates or context.

Read the current profile and principles for personalized advice. Keep approximate
dates approximate. Ask for missing facts only when they materially change the
answer. Use available research tools directly, with sources and checked dates.

Give a concise recommendation, conditions, and uncertainty. Use current preferences
rather than historical viability thresholds. Save findings under the shared research
rules. A recommendation does not confirm a trip or booking, and destination research
is not a prerequisite for trip capture. Do not start a dossier automatically.
