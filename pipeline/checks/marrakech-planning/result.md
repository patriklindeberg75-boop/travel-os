Checked September 27, 2026. This is a hypothetical exercise approved by Patrik,
not his itinerary, location, reservation or decision. October 19–21 are test dates

## Original proposal, before the test correction

Assume arrival at Marrakech Menara Airport on October 19, with time to reach a
hostel that evening. No arrival hour or onward commitment is assumed.

**October 19. Marrakech.** Stay one night in a social hostel, get local food and
meet people. Ask whether anyone wants to share the journey into the mountains.
Riad Marrakech Rouge is a candidate, subject to price and conditions below.

**October 20. Marrakech to Imlil via Asni.** Leave in the morning after confirming
the departure stand. Allow the morning for transport and waiting. Reach the
guesthouse, have lunch and ask about a short village walk and shared dinner.
Keep the afternoon optional if connections take longer.

**October 21. Imlil.** Ask the guesthouse to arrange a village-focused day walk
with other guests if possible. Choose the route and quote after checking the
forecast, trail conditions and walking demands. Sleep in the same place again
instead of combining the main walk with an uncertain last taxi.

This recommendation balances a social arrival with villages and mountain scenery.
Two Imlil nights give the walk room to move. It does not promise a group will form.

## Accommodation, social fit and budget

Riad Marrakech Rouge offers dorms, breakfast and communal spaces. On the opened
listing, Sasha's September 2026 review describes music and conversation, while
Karine's September review reports bathroom, door-lock and noise problems. It is
a social candidate with mixed evidence, not a confident booking recommendation.
The listing also gives conflicting checkout times. Confirm current conditions,
taxes, cancellation terms and the dated total; no October bed price was retrieved.

[Tamatert's own room page](https://www.atlasguest.com/our-rooms/) advertises a
Targa dorm at €12 per person or €36 for the room. Single-bed sale, inclusions,
taxes and October availability need confirmation. Its services page contains
an April 2025 guest account of meeting people and a December 2024 account of
being alone in a dorm. These are property-hosted testimonials, not independent
proof of a busy backpacker scene. Ask whether other walkers are staying and
whether dinner is communal.

Use €120 as the ordinary three-day spending target. The following is a spending
allocation, not a researched market-price forecast:

| Item | Three-day allocation |
| --- | ---: |
| Three nights, including the final Imlil night | €45 |
| Food and drinking water | €45 |
| Ordinary local movement and activities | €30 |
| Total | €120 |

If two €12 dorm nights are actually available, the bed allocation leaves €21 for
the Marrakech night including taxes. A paid guided walk belongs in ordinary
activity costs here; its quote is missing, so the €40 average is **not yet proven**.
Airport and inter-town transfers are additional and must be shown in the trip
total. With the advertised daytime airport transfer, the envelope becomes
€135 plus the unknown shared-taxi fares and final pickup, before any ordinary
spending overrun. A solo private mountain transfer adds the old €60–90 lead
instead of shared fares. Do not divide a vehicle fare by imagined companions.

## Weather, walking and opening checks

Morocco's [official weather vigilance guide](https://vigilance.marocmeteo.ma/?q=fr/meteo/guide)
covers the next 24–48 hours. It cannot establish October 19–21 conditions now.
Check Imlil's local forecast and Al Haouz warnings near departure, then ask the
host about road and path access. If conditions are poor, postpone the walk and
use an indoor meal or guesthouse day; a lower route is not automatically safe.

[Dar Adrar](https://www.daradrar.com/) advertises local day-walk arrangements.
[Atlas Peaks' June 26, 2025 walking guide](https://atlaspeaksimlil.com/imlil-day-hikes-6-best-routes-with-maps-times/)
lists a 1.5–2 hour waterfall loop and a 5–6 hour Tizi n'Mzik outing with roughly
750–850 m ascent. Those are guide estimates, not current trail verification.
For this exercise prefer a locally agreed village walk. The longer pass walk
requires explicit comfort with its demands. The two-day limit does not imply
fitness for a Toubkal summit.

The [national calendar](https://www.maroc.ma/fr/le-maroc/fetes-nationales-et-religieuses)
and [2026 holiday listing](https://www.officeholidays.com/countries/morocco)
show no national holiday on October 19–21. The national calendar includes October
31, which the ministry's separate general holiday table omits. Use the dated
check, not a blanket claim that October has no holidays. Religious dates in the
national calendar use the Hijri calendar; the secondary 2026 listing supplies
Gregorian dates. Local closures remain possible. No timed attraction is essential
to this plan; meal service, check-in and guide availability still require confirmation.
