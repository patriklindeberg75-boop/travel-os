## Start with Chez Elle Snack

My first choice for a modest food discovery is **Chez Elle Snack**, 19 boulevard
Allal Al Fassi, near Majorelle, Marrakech, Morocco. Try **rfissa**, chicken and
lentils over shredded msemen, or **seffa medfouna** if you like sweet-savory food.
Leslie Leroy, a Marrakech-based travel planner, describes it as a regular haunt
and specifically recommends those dishes in her August 2024
[firsthand guide](https://www.lespetitesjambes.com/maroc/restaurant-traditionnel-marrakech/).
Her site sells travel services and promotes tours. Meal hosting/payment is unclear.
This is a promising lead, not an independently proven best restaurant.

The current accessible [Glovo menu](https://glovoapp.com/en/ma/marrakech/stores/chez-elle-snack)
lists rfissa and seffa at **60 MAD each**. These are delivery-platform dish prices,
not a dine-in quote or a total including drinks or delivery.
[Restaurant Guru](https://restaurantguru.com/Chez-elle-snack-Marrakesh) corroborates
the address and shows recent general diner praise, but that does not verify the
rfissa specifically. Its relative review dates and hours are not a confirmed
service schedule. Ask whether the dish is available and its dine-in price.

## What the hype check changed

Chez Lamine's medina branch was an early candidate. A
[2023 firsthand account](https://thatbestbite.com/2023/01/04/lamb-tagia-and-mechoui-at-chez-lamine/)
praised its lamb dishes. But repeat visitor Kien's
[account updated August 2026](https://whereandwander.com/jemaa-el-fna-best-place-mechoui-alley/)
withdraws his recommendation following a 2025 visit, citing poor portions and value.
[Selected reviews on Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/fr/place/details/1235436/chez-lamine-hadj-mustapha-jemaa-el-fna)
also include complaints about tough meat and bone-heavy portions alongside praise.
That is enough to drop it from my preferred three, not to declare the business a
scam. The Gueliz branch is a separate identity and was not judged from medina reviews.

Amal's older **70 MAD** couscous figure appears in
[Petit Fute](https://www.petitfute.com/v48159-marrakech/c1165-restaurants/c1031-cuisines-du-monde/1528565-restaurant-de-l-association-amal.html)
and older diner reports. The venue's linked menu takes precedence at **95 MAD**.

## Useful ordering tips

- Confirm the dish, portion and total before ordering. At a grill, distinguish the
  meat-only weight price from the bill with bread, sides and drinks.
- For Jemaa el-Fna, specify any sides you want rather than accepting unexplained
  extras. Kien reports unwanted additions and higher bills in his own visits.
- Friday couscous at Amal is a concrete option. Do not infer that every restaurant
  serves couscous only on Fridays.
- Ask about today's preparation at Chez Elle. A dish listed on delivery is not a
  guarantee that it will be available for your lunch.
